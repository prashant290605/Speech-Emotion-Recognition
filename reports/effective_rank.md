# Effective rank of the source-train covariance

Per backbone, per layer, on the `source_train` split of each corpus's in-domain pair at seed 0 — the matrix an actual run fits on. Generated by `ser effective-rank`.

## Estimators

**Spectral entropy** (Roy & Vetterli 2007): `exp(-Σ pᵢ log pᵢ)` with `pᵢ = λᵢ/Σλ`. This is the estimator reported in the `cov_effective_rank` column of `results/runs.jsonl`.

**Participation ratio**: `(Σλᵢ)² / Σλᵢ²`. Weighted more towards the largest eigenvalues, so it reads lower when a few directions dominate.

Both are continuous, unlike a thresholded numerical rank, so they separate 'energy spread evenly' from 'a handful of directions carry everything' — which is what decides whether whitening is meaningful.

## Reading this

Nominal dimension is 768 throughout. Where the effective rank is a small fraction of that, CORAL is whitening a badly degenerate matrix and the shrinkage `eps` is not a numerical nicety but the thing deciding what the transform does. Layer 0 is the CNN feature-encoder output; layers 1–12 are the transformer blocks.
