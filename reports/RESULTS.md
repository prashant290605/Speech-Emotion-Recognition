|  | `mkmmd_full` |  | 40.3 | 21.4 | **0.53** |

**Label shift is negligible** (KL ≈ 0.022–0.025 nats, total variation 0.085), and the whole difference is one class: RAVDESS carries 0.231 neutral against CREMA-D's 0.146, a consequence of the `calm→neutral` mapping.

**Alignment removes the marginal term, not the conditional one.** Marginal falls to 0.010× and conditional only to 0.07×; the ratio rises from 0.14 to 0.92. What remains after alignment is conditional shift.

**Falsifiable test passes.** Near-zero prior KL predicts a label-shift correction cannot help. BBSE and SLD-EM **hurt in 238 of 240** (record, estimator) pairs, by 0.013 to 0.24 macro-F1. Both recover a *planted* prior shift to within 0.05 in `tests/test_analysis_shift.py`, so this is a fact about the corpora, not a broken implementation.

## 6. Frame dependence

Filter: 13-layer sweep, 2340 runs, logreg, 6 rungs, 3 backbones, both directions, 5 seeds. ρ computed within each seed across the 13 layers, then averaged.

| frame | pooled ρ | 95% interval |
|---|---|---|
| rung's own geometry | **-0.444** | [-0.505, -0.383] |
| fixed ZCA reference | **+0.142** | [+0.062, +0.221] |

**Opposite signs, neither interval covering zero.** 11 of 36 individual cells disagree by the stricter per-cell test (both intervals excluding zero, opposite sides).

The `eps` probe supplies the case where the correct answer is known analytically: as ε grows the CORAL map provably tends to a scalar rescale plus a mean shift, and the **reference frame** tracks `mean_shift` while the own-geometry statistic does not. See [eps_asymptote.md](eps_asymptote.md).

## 8. Retractions and corrections

Five claims were made during this rebuild and later withdrawn or narrowed. **None may be reused in the manuscript in its original form.**

| claim | status | what replaced it |
|---|---|---|
| "z-scoring removes 97.6% of marginal MMD, more than CORAL" | **retracted** | A per-rung-bandwidth artefact. Corrected: CORAL 2.0× null vs z-score 31.3× at fixed bandwidth. |
| "the cheapest step does all the work" (Stage 0, single seed) | **retracted** | Over-read of a 0.019 spread at one seed. |
| "target macro-F1 is flat across 226× of discrepancy" (Stage 0) | **refuted** | At `last`, the exact condition measured, alignment is worth +0.084 fwd / +0.128 rev at 5 seeds. |
| "`layer:6` has lower discrepancy than `last` and transfers worse" | **half refuted** | Lower discrepancy does not reproduce under the corrected MMD. The transfer half survives only unaligned, forward. |
| "in-domain-optimal depth is 4–5 layers shallower, in all three backbones" | **retracted** | Pooled gap **+0.00 layers** [−0.57, +0.57] over 36 cells. Survives only at rung `none`, forward: +4.2, +4.2, +2.4 layers. |
| "selecting depth on `source_val` systematically picks the wrong depth" | **contradicted** | Against a random layer, `source_val` is *better* by −0.0366 [−0.0448, −0.0285]. |
| "extending the eps grid would move the argmax again" | **falsified** | It saturates; in CREMA-D→RAVDESS the argmax is interior at ε=100. |

Two claims were **demoted and then partly restored**: frame dependence was promoted on 2-seed evidence, demoted when the ladder axis showed agreement, and confirmed at full seed count on the layer axis — but re-specified, since it does not hold at rung `none` where it was first seen.
