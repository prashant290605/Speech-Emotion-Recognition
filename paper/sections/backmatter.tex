\section*{Data availability}

RAVDESS \citep{livingstone2018ryerson} and CREMA-D \citep{cao2014crema} are
third-party corpora and are not redistributed. The repository contains the
manifest construction, label mapping, speaker-disjoint split derivation,
generated results, and scripts for every manuscript table and figure.

The committed artefacts are the result ledgers, a portable manifest of speakers
and labels, and the per-speaker confusion matrices of the main ledger, which
are a sufficient statistic for its cluster bootstraps. Every
table, figure, interval and reported number in this article and its supplement
can be regenerated from these files alone, without audio or models, with one
exception. The paired intervals of the two label-harmonisation controls
(Supplementary Section~S1) are computed from the per-utterance predictions of
the control runs, which are not committed. The audio, the self-supervised feature
caches and the remaining per-utterance prediction files are not committed
either, because they are third-party or large and regenerable. Repeating the
feature extraction and the grid therefore requires obtaining RAVDESS and
CREMA-D from their distributors under their own terms.

\section*{Code availability}

The code is available in the
\href{https://github.com/prashant290605/Speech-Emotion-Recognition}{public project repository}.
Its README lists the frozen result ledger, its checksum and the configuration
tag, and gives the commands that regenerate every table and figure from the
committed files. Supplementary Section~S10 describes the provenance controls,
consistency checks and tests.

\section*{Ethics statement}

This study uses existing public corpora of acted emotional speech. It collects
no new human-participant data. The reported cross-corpus scores are evidence
about evaluation methods and are not evidence for consequential affective
inference.
