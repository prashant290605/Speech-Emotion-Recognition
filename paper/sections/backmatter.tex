\section*{Data availability}

RAVDESS \citep{livingstone2018ryerson} and CREMA-D \citep{cao2014crema} are
third-party corpora and are not redistributed. The repository contains the
manifest construction, label mapping, speaker-disjoint split derivation,
generated results, and scripts for every manuscript table and figure.

What this does and does not allow is worth separating. The committed
artifacts are the result ledgers, a portable manifest of speakers and labels,
and per-speaker confusion matrices, which are a sufficient statistic for the
cluster bootstraps reported here. Every table, figure, interval and reported
number in this article and its supplement regenerates from those files alone,
with no audio and no model. What is \emph{not} committed is the audio itself,
the self-supervised feature caches and the per-utterance prediction files:
these are third-party or large and regenerable. Repeating the feature
extraction and the grid therefore requires obtaining RAVDESS and CREMA-D from
their own distributors under their own terms.

\section*{Ethics statement}

about evaluation methods and are not evidence for consequential affective
inference.
