\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Cross-corpus speech emotion recognition has long documented degradation when
training and evaluation use different corpora \cite{schuller2010cross}.
Published responses include fine-tuning \cite{naderi2023cross}, auxiliary
objectives \cite{fu2023cross}, training strategies \cite{pastor2023cross}, and
domain adaptation \cite{latif2023self,naeeni2025feature}. RAVDESS, CREMA-D,
and IEMOCAP are frequently used corpora \cite{livingstone2018ryerson,
cao2014crema,busso2008iemocap}. Differences in label mapping, splits, and
metrics prevent a numerical comparison of gains between those studies. We
therefore compare fixed alignment conditions within one protocol.

\subsection{Alignment and discrepancy}
\label{sec:related-alignment}

Covariate-shift methods assume that $P(y\mid x)$ remains stable while the
feature marginal changes \cite{shimodaira2000covariate}. Domain-adaptation
bounds use a classifier-dependent divergence
\cite{bendavid2010theory}. Kernel MMD is not that divergence; it depends on
the feature samples and its kernel specification. CORAL whitens source
covariance and recolours it with target covariance
\cite{sun2016coral,sun2016deep}. MMD methods minimise a kernel two-sample
statistic \cite{gretton2012kernel}, including multi-kernel deep adaptation
\cite{long2015dan}. Adversarial methods instead use a domain discriminator
\cite{ganin2016dann}. This study evaluates CORAL and MK-MMD as affine
feature-space transformations rather than as training losses.

\subsection{Representations and evaluation}
\label{sec:related-protocol}

HuBERT, wav2vec~2.0, and WavLM provide the frozen speech representations
\cite{hsu2021hubert,baevski2020wav2vec,chen2022wavlm}. Information differs
across transformer layers \cite{pasad2021layer}; learned layer aggregation is
therefore an experimental axis rather than a fixed default
\cite{yang2021superb}. Target labels cannot select cross-corpus configurations.
Selecting by target score produces selection bias \cite{cawley2010selection}.
We distinguish source-validation selection from the target-score oracle and
use speaker-disjoint partitions. This follows the evaluation motivation of
recent SER benchmarks \cite{ma2024emobox}.
