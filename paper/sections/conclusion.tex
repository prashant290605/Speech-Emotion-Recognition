\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A selection criterion can be leakage-free and still be uninformative. For
stationary-kernel classifiers, this gap is exact: translating the source
training and validation features together preserves every source-validation
prediction while shifting the target decision function, so additional
source-validation data cannot resolve the choice. Matched RBF-SVM runs between
RAVDESS and CREMA-D show the predicted equality in every cell, whereas
classifiers outside the fitting assumptions do not generally retain it. The
affine factorisation further shows that alignment methods ordered by matched
moments are compared as complete pipelines, and that a reported discrepancy
reduction is defined only relative to a stated basis and bandwidth. For
practitioners who select cross-corpus SER pipelines without target labels, an
identical source-validation score can therefore conceal a different target
decision function. The released audit allows this property to be checked on
recorded runs.
